Harvesting Excellence

The Kenyan "Bean to Cup" Journey

Why is Kenyan coffee considered among the finest in the world? Discover our time-honored double-washed method and sun drying on raised African beds.

01

Selective Cherry Pick

Farmers hand-select only deep crimson, fully ripe cherries on slopes of Mt. Kenya, ensuring peak natural sugar levels.

02

Double Fermentation

Wet-milled using fresh mountain streams, followed by a unique 24-72hr double fermentation that yields signature winey acidity.

03

African Raised Beds

Dried slowly under the high-altitude equatorial sun on elevated bamboo tables to maintain moisture equilibrium.

04

Precision Micro-Roast

Roasted in light-to-medium profiles to highlight sparkling blackcurrant notes, delicate jasmine florals, and complex sugar development.
